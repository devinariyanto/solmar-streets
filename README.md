**Solmar Streets** is a complete open-world crime game in the browser: a sun-soaked fictional coastal city where you play a garage courier taking on the harbour extortion ring to save the family workshop. Walk the promenade, steal cars, outrun the police, fight the harbour crew, and finish five story missions — then keep the city in free roam.

- **A living city** — harbour, downtown grid, beach promenade and lighthouse; traffic that drives the road grid, pedestrians who walk the pavements and flee gunfire, a day-lit sea that moves.
- **On foot and by car** — third-person walking and sprinting, arcade driving with handbrake slides, entering and stealing parked cars, vehicle reset.
- **Crime → wanted → chase → escape** — stealing and shooting raise stars; police cars and foot cops hunt you; break line of sight and stay quiet to cool the heat down.
- **Five story missions** — a courier's fight against the extortion ring, with briefings, objectives, a beacon and minimap marker, retries, rewards, an ending with stats and credits, and free roam after.
- **Combat with consequences** — a working pistol (aim, fire, reload, hit markers), enemies who close in, strafe and shoot back, health and regeneration.
- **HUD + minimap + save** — cash, wanted stars, health, ammo, speed, a live minimap, dialogs, and progress saved in localStorage.
- **Synthesised sound** — engine hum, police sirens, gunshots and the sea, all built with WebAudio. No audio files, no network: the whole game runs offline from static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk/drive, Shift to sprint, mouse to look/aim, click to shoot, E to enter cars/talk/pick up, R to reload, Space for handbrake, F to reset the car, P to pause, M to mute.
